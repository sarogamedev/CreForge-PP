# Privacy Policy for CreForge

**Last Updated: March 15, 2026**

Saravana Balaji ("the Developer," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information in the CreForge mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
CreForge is an **offline-first** application. 
*   **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, or location, to our servers or any third parties.
*   **Local Storage:** All data you input into the App—including client details, task descriptions, idea vault entries, business information, and signatures—is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database. 
*   **No Tracking:** We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any other tracking technologies to monitor your activity.

## 2. Data Security
Because all your data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on the security of your mobile device. We recommend using device-level security features such as passcodes, biometric locks, and encryption to protect your information.

## 3. Third-Party Services
*   **PDF Generation:** The App uses local libraries to generate PDF invoices. This process happens entirely on your device.
*   **External Sharing:** When you choose to share a PDF invoice via other apps (e.g., email, messaging apps), the data contained in that PDF is handled by the third-party app you select. We do not have control over how those apps handle your data.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at thesaro47@gmail.com

---

**Note:** Since this app is 100% offline, your data belongs to you. If you delete the App, all locally stored data will be permanently removed unless you have manually backed up your device.
